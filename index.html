These are not marketing lines — they are the reasons some features never make it into our games.

    From idea to store

    How a Bhushan game gets made

    1. 01

      One mechanic, tested to death

      A prototype has to be fun with placeholder art and no sound. If it needs polish to be interesting, the idea gets shelved.

    2. 02

      Levels made by hand

      Every level is authored, solved and difficulty-rated by a person before it ships. Generated content is cheap, and it plays cheap.

    3. 03

      Tuned for the phone you own

      We test on entry-level hardware first. Under 25 MB, instant cold start and a steady frame rate on devices from 2017.

    4. 04

      Shipped, then kept alive

      Closed testing, staged rollout, then monthly content. Bug reports are read and answered by the person who wrote the code.
