A small studio with a short list of rules

Bhushan Games builds puzzle and casual games for Android. We are not trying to be the biggest studio on the store — we are trying to make the two or three games you keep installed after the rest get deleted.

Founded 2024 Based in India Platform Android

Why we started

Most mobile puzzle games are built to extract time rather than reward it. Lives that refill on a timer, a video ad between every board, a pop-up asking you to rate the game before you have finished level three. It works, commercially — and it makes games nobody keeps.

We started Bhushan Games in 2024 with the opposite bet: build something small, quiet and genuinely good, put it on the store for free, and let it stay on people's phones. Merge 2248 crossed its first 50,000 installs without a rupee of paid marketing. That is the whole strategy.

We are a tiny team. The person who writes the code also answers the support emails, which is the main reason bug reports get fixed instead of filed.
